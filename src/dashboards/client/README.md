# Client Dashboard Module

## Responsibility & Boundaries
The **Client Dashboard** provides client athletes with access to their personalized training portal.

### Key Capabilities
- **Active Training Journey**: Live status of assigned UK performance coach, upcoming strategy session date & time, and meeting links.
- **Workout Programs**: Access customized workout programs, exercise video tutorials, and daily training logs.
- **Nutrition Blueprint**: Target calorie budget, protein/macro breakdown, meal schedule, and hydration logs.
- **Bookings & Sessions**: Confirmed 1-on-1 strategy sessions and class bookings.
- **Body Metrics**: Body weight tracking, BMI calculations, and transformation progress.
- **Subscriptions & Billing**: Active membership pass status and payment receipts.
- **Support Tickets**: Raise support requests directly with BxStrength support.
