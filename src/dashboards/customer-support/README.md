# Customer Support Dashboard Module

## Responsibility & Boundaries
The **Customer Support Dashboard** provides client service specialists with ticket handling, inquiry responses, and escalation workflows.

### Key Capabilities
- **Support Ticket Management**: Respond to client tickets, update ticket statuses, and assign priority levels.
- **Inquiry Queue**: Review incoming contact form submissions and client health queries.
- **Senior Escalation**: Escalate complex tickets to Head Coach or Executive Admin.
- **SLA & Resolution Analytics**: Track average response time and open vs resolved ticket metrics.
